============================================================
GUI STRUCTURE
============================================================

This GUI is designed to explore OMOP clinical data and
waveform-derived features on a patient basis.

------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

Quick summary of the dataset:

- Patients
- Events
- Concepts
- General statistics

------------------------------------------------------------
PATIENT EXPLORER
------------------------------------------------------------

Patient summary page.

Shows:

- Patient IDs
- Demographics
- Data availability
- Visit summary

Useful to understand what data exists for a patient.

------------------------------------------------------------
CLINICAL TIMELINE
------------------------------------------------------------

Chronological view of the patient's hospital history.

Includes:

- Admissions
- Transfers
- Diagnoses
- Procedures
- Medications
- Clinical measurements

Use the checkboxes to show or hide event types.

------------------------------------------------------------
WAVEFORM ANALYTICS
------------------------------------------------------------

Dedicated page for waveform-derived features.

Examples:

- HRV
- RMSSD
- SDNN
- Respiratory Rate
- Blood Pressure
- Pleth features

------------------------------------------------------------
MEDICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

List of medication administrations for the selected patient.

------------------------------------------------------------
CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

List of diagnoses and clinical conditions.

------------------------------------------------------------
DOCTOR NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

Clinical notes and reports associated with the patient.

------------------------------------------------------------
SUGGESTED WORKFLOW
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Select a patient
2. Open Patient Explorer
3. Review the Clinical Timeline
4. Check Medications and Conditions
5. Read Doctor Notes
6. Explore Waveform Analytics

============================================================
